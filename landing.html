Features

Everything a service business needs

Stop paying for HoneyBook + Dubsado + ChatGPT + Calendly + Notion + e-sign. One subscription, one login, one place where your work happens.

Private client portals

Each client gets a unique link. They review documents, sign contracts (typed or finger-drawn), pay invoices, and message you — all without an account.

11 contract templates

Service Agreement, NDA, Independent Contractor, Coaching, Retainer, SOW, Website, Copyright, Release, Speaker, Client Contract — generate, edit, send, and sign in one click.

AI specialists, built-in

Eight purpose-built AI agents for business strategy, legal docs, credit, content, programs, outreach, and creative ideation. No separate ChatGPT subscription needed.

Vibe Coder

Describe a website to the AI in plain English. It builds a real, working HTML/CSS/JS app in your browser with file explorer, live preview, and a one-click ship-to-portal handoff.

Stripe + BNPL payments

Built-in Stripe Checkout for invoices. Klarna and Afterpay are already supported through Stripe — toggle them on, your clients see them at checkout.

Calendar that subscribes

Add events to your dashboard calendar. Subscribe to your personal iCal feed in Google, Apple, or Outlook — events sync to all your devices automatically.

White-label your brand

Upload your logo, set your five-color brand palette, use your own domain. Clients see your business name on every portal and document — never ours.

Email that lands

Resend integration sends portal links, contracts, and proposals from your verified business domain. Replies come straight to your inbox.

Breakdowns & AI assistant

Save step-by-step explainers for ADHD-friendly reference. The AI Assistant has tool access to your real business data — read clients, write notes, schedule events by chat.
