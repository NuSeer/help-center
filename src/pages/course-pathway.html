Course Development

Plan, build, package, and launch a paid course or learning program

My Course

Validate before you build

Before recording a single video: get 5 conversations with people who match your target student. Ask what they have already tried, what they have spent money on, and what they wish existed. If 3 of 5 will pre-pay or sign a wait-list, you have validation. If not, refine the promise.

Modules & Lessons

Sketch out your modules and the lessons inside each. Aim for 4–8 modules with 3–5 lessons per module.

Backwards design works

Start from the final outcome and work backwards. What does a student need to know in week 4? What scaffolding did week 3 require? What did week 2 unlock? Most weak courses are weak because the order does not build.

Editing & Delivery

Done is better than perfect — but record the first lesson three times

The first lesson always sounds awkward. Record it on day 1, again on day 7, and again at the end of production. Use the third take. Everyone you teach will have a more polished first impression.

Pricing Tiers

Most courses sell better with 2–3 price tiers. Anchor with the highest, drive volume with the middle.

Beta cohort pricing rule

Run the first cohort at 50% off in exchange for testimonials, case studies, and feedback. Make this explicit: students get a discount, you get social proof and a chance to fix the curriculum before the full-price launch.

Launch Plan — 4 Week Sequence

Cart-close beats launch-day for sales

The last 24 hours of a launch typically deliver 30–50% of total sales. Tell your audience you are closing, mean it, and close on time. Re-opening early trains buyers to wait.

Pick the platform that matches your delivery

Self-paced video → Teachable / Thinkific / Podia. Cohort with community → Skool or Kajabi. Live workshop series → Zoom + a private Slack/Discord. Do not overpay for features you will not use in the first launch.
