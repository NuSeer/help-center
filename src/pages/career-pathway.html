Job Search Toolkit

Resume Power Checklist

Lead with impact numbers — "Increased revenue by 34%" beats "Responsible for sales"
Tailor for each application — mirror the job description's keywords (ATS systems scan for matches)
One page for under 10 years experience — two pages maximum for senior roles
Strong summary section at top — 2–3 sentences positioning you for the specific role
No objectives, no references available upon request — both are outdated

LinkedIn Optimization

Professional headshot — profiles with photos get 21x more views
Headline beyond your job title — "Operations Leader helping teams scale without burning out"
About section in first person — tell your story, not a job description
Request 3–5 recommendations — from managers, clients, and peers
Turn on "Open to Work" — for passive searches, keep it visible to recruiters only

Networking Strategy

Reconnect with 5 former colleagues — most opportunities come from "weak ties," not close friends
Request 2 informational interviews per month — "coffee chats" at target companies
Join 1–2 professional associations — in your target industry or function
