Origin

Built from the work, not the theory.

H.E.L.P. Center began in January 2024 as a one-woman consultancy serving Black-owned and women-owned small businesses — credit repair, formation, branding, websites. The system you see today is the playbook that worked, codified.

Who It Serves

Everyday Leaders.

Coaches, consultants, side-hustlers, parents reentering work, entrepreneurs at the kitchen table. People building something real with whatever time and tools they have.

What Makes It Different

One dashboard, not seven tabs.

  • Coaching curriculum + CRM + invoicing in one place.
  • Six pathways that cross-link — credit feeds business, career feeds confidence.
  • AI coach trained on the H.E.L.P. method, not generic advice.
  • Mobile-first; survives airplane mode and tab suspends.
